## Improved
- New accounts now get relays for private messages that are working today: auth.nostr1.com and nos.lol
- Relay details now show when sign-in isn't working on a relay
- Logs now record when the app is opened and left, to help explain dropped connections

## Fixed
- Once a relay refuses a sign-in, Struthio stops asking until the next app restart, so your signer app isn't asked again and again
- Relays that turn Struthio away, such as members-only relays, are now left alone for 30 minutes instead of being retried every few seconds
