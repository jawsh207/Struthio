## New
- **Profile banners:** a profile's banner now runs across the top of the screen, behind the back button, with the profile picture sitting half over its edge. Set yours in Edit profile
- **Websites:** profiles show their website, and tapping it opens it. Add yours in Edit profile
- **Lightning addresses:** profiles show their lightning address in plain name@domain form. Tap it, or the zap button, to zap them. The zap button only appears when there's an address to send to
- **Profile tabs:** switch a profile between Posts, Replies, Boosts and Gallery. Gallery shows every picture and video they've posted in a grid, with sensitive posts kept covered

## Improved
- **Working links everywhere:** links, hashtags and mentions in profile bios, private messages and relay descriptions can now be tapped, just like in notes
- **Cleaner profile top bar:** the name is no longer repeated next to the back button. The bar turns solid once you scroll past the banner
