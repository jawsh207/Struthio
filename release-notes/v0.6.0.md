## New
- Embedded videos: play YouTube, PeerTube, Invidious, Piped, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Streamable and Odysee videos inside notes. Turn it on in Settings → Feeds. Nothing loads from the video site until you tap play
- A tidier Settings screen: settings are grouped into rows with icons, and your profile card sits at the top

## Fixed
- Your connected wallet no longer disappears when its relay can't be reached. If the wallet doesn't answer, you'll see a message with a Retry button instead
- Videos stop playing when you open another screen
