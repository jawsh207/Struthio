## Fixed
- Posts no longer get stuck and then go out later. Before, a post could fail to send but quietly go out later, so trying again posted it twice
- Trying again after a failed post can't create a duplicate. Struthio resends the same post instead of making a new copy

## Improved
- When no relay answers, Struthio now says so ("No relay answered in time") instead of naming one relay that happened to be down
- Copied logs show what each relay did when a post fails
