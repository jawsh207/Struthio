## New
- **Relay data and uptime:** your relay list now shows how much data each relay has used, down and up, and how often it could be reached, over the last 30 days. Open a relay for the details

## Fixed
- **Private messages after reconnecting:** a message that arrived while you were away could be missed, because private messages carry a deliberately made-up time up to two days back. Struthio now looks back far enough to catch them
- **"Not answering" in the relay list:** relays often showed as not answering when they were fine, because the check waited behind everything else Struthio was loading. Each relay is now checked on its own
- **Relays that are gone:** relays that no longer exist, or never answer, are left alone for a while instead of being tried every few minutes, saving data and battery
- **Linked accounts:** you can paste a whole gist address for GitHub, not just its ID. Tapping a linked account now opens its proof, so anyone can check it. If a GitHub check fails, Troubleshooting logs say why
- **Edit profile** no longer re-sends your profile when only your status or linked accounts changed
