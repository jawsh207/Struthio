## Improved
- Struthio reconnects right away when your phone switches between Wi-Fi and mobile data, instead of waiting up to 12 seconds for each connection to time out
- Anything you were sending during a switch goes out again on the new connection
- When there's no network at all, Struthio waits for it to come back instead of failing every relay one by one
- Your wallet balance and history wait for the network to come back instead of failing straight away. Payments still never resend on their own
