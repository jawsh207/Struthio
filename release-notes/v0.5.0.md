## New
- Polls: create a poll from the composer, vote, and see results. Polls can be single or multiple choice and can end after 1, 3 or 7 days. Only apps that support polls will show them
- Custom emoji now show in notes, names, profile bios, reactions and notifications
- Encrypted key backup: if your key is stored in Struthio, you can export it protected by a password, then restore it by pasting the backup into the sign-in box. Nobody can recover a lost password
