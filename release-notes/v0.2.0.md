## New
- Notifications now show a person's verified NIP-05 address when they have one, instead of a long npub
- Video links with no file extension now play inside the note: Struthio asks the server what the link is, and images are recognized the same way. The Link previews setting turns this off

## Improved
- Struthio now recognizes many more kinds of video link, including MOV, MKV, 3GP, MPEG, AVI, WMV and FLV files and HLS and DASH streams. Anything your phone can't play opens in another app
