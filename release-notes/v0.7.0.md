## New
- **Picture posts:** posts made for pictures (from apps like Olas) now show in your feeds, with their title and every picture
- **Video posts:** dedicated video posts now show with their title, thumbnail and length, and play right in the post. Short vertical videos get a taller frame
- **Articles:** long-form articles now show in feeds as a card with picture, title, summary and reading time. Open one to read it in full, with headings, lists, quotes, code and images. You can comment on articles, and links to articles open from anywhere in Struthio
- **Statuses:** see what people are up to, or listening to, under their name on their profile. Set or clear your own in Edit profile
- **Linked accounts:** profiles show the other accounts people have linked, like GitHub, Mastodon or Bluesky. GitHub links that check out get a tick. Link your own in Edit profile, which shows the exact text to post as proof
- **Sign in with a remote signer:** keep your key in a signer like nsec.app or your own bunker. Paste its bunker:// link, or scan the code Struthio shows with your signer
- **Delete account everywhere:** in Settings, under Sign out, you can ask every relay to permanently erase everything you've posted. It can't be undone, so it asks you to type DELETE first
