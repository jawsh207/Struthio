## New
- Swipe between tabs. Going back from any tab returns to Home, and going back from Home leaves the app
- Messages and notifications now stay unread until you open them, with a dot and an unread count where it helps
- Mark all as read: a button on the Messages and Notifications pages, handy for things you've already seen in another app
- Copy logs: a new Advanced section in Settings lets you copy logs when reporting a problem. They leave out your keys and the text of your notes and messages

## Improved
- Message times now sit below the bubble, with a date divider for each day
- Relay details now show "Signed in" once a relay has accepted your sign-in

## Fixed
- A relay asking you to sign in no longer shows up as a problem, and old connection errors clear once the relay reconnects
- Notifications from muted people no longer count toward the badge
