## New
- Starting a new message now works like search: type a name, NIP-05 address or npub and pick the person from the list. People you follow appear first
- Search can now look up a NIP-05 address directly and open that person's profile
- People lists now show the verified badge, so a real account is easy to tell apart from one copying its address

## Improved
- Images should load faster: pictures already on your phone appear right away, and avatars no longer hold up pictures in notes
- Videos now show a spinner while they load or buffer
